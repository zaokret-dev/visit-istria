Who we are

Try Istria (tryistria.com) helps guests book boat tours, rentals, activities and accommodation in and around Pula, Croatia. We are responsible for the personal data you send us through this website, by WhatsApp or by e-mail.

Contact for anything about your data: info@tryistria.com, phone +385 91 55 77 230, Riva, 52100 Pula, Croatia.

What data we receive

We only receive what you choose to send us. Usually that is:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number or e-mail address
  • The date, the service and the number of adults and children
  • Anything you write in your message (for example a question about pets or accessibility)
We never ask for ID numbers, passport details or card numbers through this website.

Why we use it

We use your data to answer your question, check availability and arrange your booking (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR – steps before and for performing a contract). We keep booking records where Croatian law requires it, for example for accounting (Article 6(1)(c)). We may keep our correspondence for a limited time to handle complaints or questions about a past booking (Article 6(1)(f) – legitimate interest).

We do not send newsletters or advertising, and we never sell your data.

Who else sees it

Most trips and stays are run by local partners: boat operators, skippers, dive centres and accommodation owners. When you book, we pass on only what they need to provide the service – usually your name, phone number, date and group size.

Messages reach us through the services you choose: WhatsApp (Meta), e-mail (our address is hosted with Google and routed through Cloudflare) and our website host, GitHub Pages, which keeps short technical logs such as IP addresses. Some of these companies may process data outside the EU under the safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The enquiry form stores nothing on the website: it simply opens WhatsApp or your e-mail app with your message ready to send.

The map on our pages loads from Google only after you click on it. From that moment Google’s own privacy policy applies.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months. Booking details are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and handle any complaints, and accounting records for as long as Croatian law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to limit how we use it or to send it to you in a common format. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to info@tryistria.com and we will reply within one month.

Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, please tell us first – we will try to fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Croatian data protection authority: Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Send us your dates and group size. We check availability and reply within 24 hours.
