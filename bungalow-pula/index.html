A quiet, private stay near the pine-shaded coves of southern Pula.

Peace, privacy and the sea nearby

The bungalow sits in a quiet residential part of Pula, known for its Mediterranean feel, pine trees and clear sea. It feels more like a small holiday house than a standard apartment, with its own terrace and garden.

Location

The Arena and the old town are a short drive away. Marina Veruda, one of the largest marinas on the south Istrian coast, is close by, and Pula Airport is about 10 km away, roughly 15 minutes by car.

Beaches nearby

The south of Pula is known for rocky coves and small beaches among pines. Ambrela Beach and the coves of the Verudela peninsula are a short drive away, with clear water and natural shade.

Comfort

  • Small garden area with shade
  • Free private parking next to the property
  • Air conditioning

Frequently asked questions

How is the bungalow different from an apartment?
It feels more like a small holiday house: your own terrace, a shaded garden and parking next to the door, in a quiet residential street.
Is it a good base for divers and sailors?
Yes. Marina Veruda is close by, and Pula is known for diving, so both are within easy reach.
Can we have breakfast outside?
Yes. The private terrace is made for breakfast in the morning and drinks in the evening, and the garden has shade.
What can we do nearby in the evening?
Swim at Ambrela Beach or in the Verudela coves before sunset, or take the sunset dolphin tour from the Riva.
