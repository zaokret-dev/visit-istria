About Try Istria: a small team on the Riva in Pula

We organise the trips we would want to take ourselves: honest times, simple prices and enough room to enjoy the sea.

What we do

Try Istria brings together the things visitors usually want to do around Pula: a loop around the Brijuni Islands with time for a swim, an evening on the water, a day in Rovinj or Venice, a quiet morning in a kayak, a dive, a boat of your own and a comfortable place to sleep.

Instead of five separate websites and five separate e-mails, you write to one team. We tell you what is possible on your dates and put the pieces in the right order.

Our boats leave from the Riva, near ACI Marina.

How a booking works

  1. 1

    You write to us

    Send your date, group size and what you have in mind, by WhatsApp, e-mail or the form.

  2. 2

    We check

    We confirm availability and answer any questions within 24 hours.

  3. 3

    You get the details

    Meeting point, time and what to bring, in one clear message.

  4. 4

    You come to the Riva

    Arrive 30 minutes before departure and we take it from there.

Somewhere to sleep

Guests often ask us where to stay, so we also offer apartments in Pula and Fažana and a quiet bungalow near Marina Veruda. Both are a convenient base for boat days and for exploring the coast by car.
