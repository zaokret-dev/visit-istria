Trip one: Rovinj and Vrsar

Rovinj is one of the most photographed towns on the Istrian coast: pastel houses stacked up a small peninsula, narrow cobbled lanes and the bell tower of the Church of St. Euphemia above it all. On the way we also stop in nearby Vrsar.

  • Time: 09:00–18:00
  • Past the Brijuni Islands and Lim Bay, then two hours in Vrsar and two hours in Rovinj
  • Lunch on board (grilled fish or chicken, salad, bread) with unlimited wine, water and juice
  • Licensed guide speaking Croatian, English, German and Italian
  • Price: 50 € per person; children 6–14 pay half, under 6 free
  • Season: Easter to October. On some dates the boat stops in Poreč instead of Vrsar
02

Trip two: Venice

Venice is built on 118 small islands linked by more than 400 bridges. Highlights include St. Mark’s Basilica, the Grand Canal, the Rialto Bridge and the Doge’s Palace. A fast catamaran takes you there and back, which leaves you about five hours in the city.

  • Departure from Pula at 07:30, back around 20:30
  • About 3.5–4 hours each way by fast catamaran
  • About five hours of free time in Venice
  • Several days a week, from June to mid-September
  • Price: 85 € in June and September, 95 € in July and August; children pay half, small children travel free
  • Any Venice entry fee for day visitors is not included

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a passport for the Venice trip?
Bring a valid passport or ID card. Ask us if you are unsure which document your nationality needs.
Is a guide included?
On the Rovinj and Vrsar trip, yes: a licensed guide is on board and speaks Croatian, English, German and Italian. The Venice trip is a catamaran crossing with free time in the city. Ask us if you would like a guided walk in Venice.
Are meals included?
On the Rovinj and Vrsar trip, lunch is served on board, with unlimited wine, water and juice. On the Venice trip, you eat in town during your free time.
When do the trips run?
Rovinj and Vrsar from Easter to October. Venice several days a week from June to mid-September. Both depend on the weather, so we confirm your date directly with you.
Can I do both trips?
Yes, but they run as separate full-day trips. Tell us your dates and we help you plan.

Send us your dates and group size. We check availability and reply within 24 hours.
