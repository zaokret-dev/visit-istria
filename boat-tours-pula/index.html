Boat tours in Pula

Two ways to see the Brijuni Islands: a long afternoon with a swim, or a three-hour evening cruise into the sunset. Meal and unlimited drinks are included.

Pick your tour

Both tours leave from the Riva in Pula and circle the Brijuni archipelago. The difference is what you do with the time: the day tour is built around a long swim, the sunset tour around the light.

Day tour with swimming

12:00–16:30 · 50 € per adult. A panoramic cruise around Brijuni and two hours at St. Jerolim. About 4.5 hours in total, well suited to families and swimmers.

Sunset tour with dolphins

18:00–21:00 · 50 € per adult. An evening cruise of about three hours in the golden light, with a chance to see dolphins. No swimming stop and no park fee.

How the day tour unfolds

  1. 12:00

    Boarding and departure

    We leave the Riva and head out towards the islands.

  2. Cruise

    Around Brijuni

    A slow, panoramic loop with commentary from the guide.

  3. 2 h

    Swimming at St. Jerolim

    Two hours to swim, snorkel and relax in clear water.

  4. On board

    Meal and drinks

    Fish, burger or a vegetarian dish, with wine, juices and water.

  5. 16:30

    Back on the Riva

    We return to Pula at the end of the afternoon.

The day tour stops at St. Jerolim, part of Brijuni National Park. A small park entrance fee is paid on the island, so keep a little cash handy. Ask us for the current amount when you book.

How the sunset tour unfolds

We leave in the early evening, sail around Brijuni and watch for dolphins while the light turns gold. There is a meal, drinks and music on deck, and the trip lasts about three hours, until 21:00.

The sunset tour start can move by up to 30 minutes with the sunset. We confirm the exact time when you book.

What is included

  • One meal per person: fish, burger or vegetarian
  • Unlimited wine, juices and mineral water
  • Music and a relaxed atmosphere on board
  • A guide who speaks several languages

Frequently asked questions

Which tour is better on a very hot day?
The day tour. You spend two hours at St. Jerolim, so there is plenty of time to cool off in the sea. The sunset tour runs after the worst heat, which suits guests who prefer the evening.
Does everyone have to swim at St. Jerolim?
No. The stop is free time: swim, snorkel, walk along the shore or simply relax. Guests who do not swim enjoy it just as much.
Should I bring cash on the day tour?
Yes, a little. The national park fee for St. Jerolim is paid on the island. Ask us for the current amount when you book.
Why can the start of the sunset tour move?
Because it follows the sun. Depending on the month, departure can shift by up to 30 minutes. We always confirm the exact time when you book.
How do we get a good place on board?
Come to the Riva 30 minutes before departure. Guests who come early choose where they sit.
I get seasick easily. Can I still join?
Yes. Take your usual remedy before departure and sit in the middle of the boat, where it moves the least. If the sea is too rough for a pleasant trip, we contact you before the tour.
