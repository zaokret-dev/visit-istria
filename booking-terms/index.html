Who does what

Try Istria helps you choose and book services in and around Pula. Most of them – boat tours, boat and yacht rental, kayaking, diving and accommodation – are provided by local partners (the operator). The operator carries out the service and is responsible for it; we pass on your booking, confirm the details and stay in touch with you. The confirmation you receive names the operator.

Booking and confirmation

You can send a booking request by WhatsApp, e-mail or the enquiry form. A request is not yet a booking. Your booking is confirmed only when we send you a written confirmation with the date, time, meeting point, price and operator.

Prices and payment

Prices on this website are in euro per person unless stated otherwise. Child prices and rules for pets are shown on each service page. Prices can change before your booking is confirmed; the price in your confirmation does not change.

How and when you pay is stated in your confirmation. For boat tours you usually pay on the day, before boarding. Boat and yacht rental and accommodation may require a deposit.

If you cancel

  • Boat tours and activities: free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure.
  • Later cancellation or not showing up: the operator may charge up to the full price.
  • Boat and yacht rental and accommodation: the cancellation rules in your confirmation apply.
Please cancel as early as possible – by WhatsApp or e-mail – so the place can go to another guest.

Weather and cancellation by the operator

Safety comes first. The captain or operator decides whether a trip can go out and may change the route or the timing because of wind, waves or national park rules. If a trip is cancelled by the operator, you can choose a new date or get a full refund of anything you have paid. If a trip has to be cut short, a fair partial refund is agreed with the operator.

On board and safety

  • Follow the instructions of the crew at all times.
  • Swimming stops are at your own risk; swim only where and when the crew allows it.
  • Parents or guardians are responsible for their children on board and in the water.
  • Life jackets are on board for every passenger.
  • We watch dolphins from a distance and do not feed them or swim with them.

Liability

The operator is responsible for carrying out the service safely and as described, in line with Croatian law and its insurance. Try Istria is responsible for passing on your booking correctly and for the information we give you. Neither of us is liable for events outside anyone’s control, such as storms, strikes or official closures. Please keep an eye on your personal belongings.

Complaints

If something goes wrong, tell the crew or the operator straight away so it can be fixed on the spot. You can also send a written complaint to info@tryistria.com within 8 days of the service. We confirm receipt and reply within 15 days.

Right of withdrawal

Bookings for leisure services on a specific date – such as boat tours, rentals and accommodation – are not covered by the 14-day right of withdrawal for distance contracts. The cancellation rules above apply instead.

Law and disputes

These terms are governed by Croatian law. We try to solve every disagreement directly and fairly. If that is not possible, the competent court in Pula decides, without affecting the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country of residence.
